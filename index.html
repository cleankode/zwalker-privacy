ZWalker privacy notice

Effective: 2026-10-23
App: ZWalker (com.cleankode.zwalker)
Price: Free. No account.

Who we are

ZWalker is published by the developer named on the store listing. Contact: coderul@proton.me.

What the app does

ZWalker counts steps, active minutes, and an estimated distance on your phone. It stores that history on the phone. Optional reminders share one notification.

What stays on your phone

There is no account. Step totals, goals, streaks, settings, and a technical log of up to 50 events stay on the phone. The app does not use GPS. A bike ride or drive your phone recognizes is never counted — no steps, no walked time. Steps it cannot recognize (for example with the app closed) still count toward your step total, but never earn walked time.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
MotionCount steps, and drop drives and bike rides as best the phone can tell.
NotificationsOptional reminders, only if you allow them.

There is no ongoing tracking notification. Steps and running activity stay on the phone, and counting continues while the app is closed — Android wakes the app briefly on activity changes instead. Turn motion off in Settings to stop counting.

You can refuse either and still open the app.

What can leave the phone

Crash reports are off until you turn them on. If you turn them on, a technical crash report is sent to Sentry. It is meant to include the app version, device model, OS version, and a scrubbed error. It is not meant to include your steps, your history, or an identifier that picks out your phone. There are no ads. Share diagnostics sends the on-device log only when you tap it and choose where it goes.

How long it is kept

History stays until you delete it or uninstall. Delete all data erases the on-device database. It does not reach a crash report already sent.

Children

ZWalker is not directed at children under 13.
